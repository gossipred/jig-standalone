Role controls what this account can view or change. 角色會決定這個帳號可以查看或修改哪些資料。

Role Guide 角色選擇說明

ADMIN
User management and scrap approval, plus normal jig editing work. 可管理帳號與核准報廢，也可進行一般治具維護。
SUPERVISOR
Team-wide jig editing and delete permission, but no user management. 可跨人修改與刪除治具，但不能管理帳號。
ENGINEER
Can add/edit jig content and update non-scrap status or due date. 可新增/修改治具內容，並更新非報廢狀態與到期日。
OPERATOR
Read-only access for daily production lookup, download, and export. 唯讀查詢、下載與匯出，適合產線日常使用。
