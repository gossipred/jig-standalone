Daily Workflow 日常使用步驟

Step 1

Search Jigs查詢治具

Search by Jig No., model, customer, or assembly line to find the right fixture quickly. 依治具編號、機種、客戶或組裝線別快速查找。

Step 2

Check Status確認狀態

Use color badges to identify On Process, Normal, Repair, Maintain, Hold, and Scrap items at a glance. 透過顏色快速辨識在線使用、正常存放、維修、保養、保留與報廢。

Step 3

Open Files查看附件

Download jig photos, drawings, PDFs, and CAD files from the detail page. 在明細頁下載治具照片、圖紙、PDF 或 CAD 檔。

Step 4

Review Logs查看紀錄

Review who changed status, due date, files, or master data and when it happened. 查看何人、何時修改狀態、到期日、附件或主資料。

Role Guide 權限說明

ADMIN

Manage users and approve Scrap.管理使用者與報廢核准。

SUPERVISOR

Edit and delete jigs across users.可跨使用者修改與刪除治具。

ENGINEER

Add and update owned jigs.新增與修改自己建立的治具。

OPERATOR

Search, view logs, and download files.查詢、看紀錄與下載檔案。
